How it behaves automatically
* Auto-pauses at midnight IST when the daily budget is exhausted
* Auto-resumes within 60 seconds after the new day's budget resets at midnight

* Skips already-covered ranges — the nightly EOD sync and the backfill share the same coverage tracking (backfill_progress table), so they never duplicate a request
* Skips invalid symbols — permanently marks them in the DB so they're never retried
